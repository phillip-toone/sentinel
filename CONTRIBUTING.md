# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Sentinel.

Sentinel emphasizes thoughtful engineering over rapid implementation.

Before contributing, please become familiar with:

- README.md
- PROJECT_CONTEXT.md
- Vision.md
- ADR-0001

General principles:

- Architecture before implementation.
- One logical change per commit.
- Document important decisions.
- Prefer simple designs.
- Keep the scoring engine platform independent.
